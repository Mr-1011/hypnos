Use when the person speaks, asleep or half asleep, and their words are about a thing that weighs on them.

1. Find what the words are about: the "(memory)" line under them, your notes, and what the band heard today.
2. Take the earlier time in the "(memory)" line that is the same kind of thing and went well. Only if none fits: `recall`, with a few words for the kind of thing.
3. Say one or two short sentences. First the earlier time: how long ago it was and how it went. Then that this one can go the same way.
4. If `recall` gives nothing that went well, say one calm sentence about the thing itself.
