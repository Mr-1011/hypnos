Use when a cue says that the body does not rest or that something disturbs the sleep, and the person says nothing.

1. The cue has the body's numbers. Your notes, what the band heard today, the calendar, the mail and the other connected tools say what comes tomorrow and what weighs on the person: take the likeliest one.
2. Find an earlier time when the same kind of thing went well: `recall`.
3. Say one short sentence that gives the earlier time and ties it to what comes. The deeper the sleep, the fewer the words.
4. If nothing weighs on them, or `recall` gives nothing that went well, say one calm sentence. In deep sleep use `stay_quiet`.
