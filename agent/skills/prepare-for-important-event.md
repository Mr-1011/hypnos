Use for an unsolicited bedtime or drowsy cue, or an awake/drowsy restlessness cue.

1. Check the stage in the cue, or call `body` if it is missing. Only proceed while awake or drowsy.
2. Identify a concrete upcoming event and the person's own statement of why it matters greatly. Routine errands, a full calendar and somebody else's worries do not qualify. If the stakes or speaker attribution are unclear, use `stay_quiet`.
3. Call `recall` for that kind of event. Require a specific helpful behavior the person said they used, not just praise, a positive outcome, or advice from someone else.
4. Say one or two brief sentences, at most fifteen words each: the earlier action, then an optional connection to the upcoming event. For example: "You paused and explained the numbers clearly last time. That same pause is available tomorrow."
5. Do not ask for rehearsal, introduce a new belief, guarantee success or keep the person talking. Do this at most once per event in the current conversation. If there is no qualifying event or memory, use `stay_quiet`.

Mock memories remain mock evidence for a demonstration. Never describe them as real recordings.
